# Universal Language Model

**Cleiton-Moura-Loura-Papers · 0002 · trilingual by conception**

*Languages:* [Português](pt.md) · [English](en.md) · [中文](zh.md)

---

We replace the concept of *Large Language Model* with **Universal Language Model** (ULM).

It is not a synonym. It is a change of telos.

## What “large” names

*Large* measures scale: parameters, tokens, data centres, capital, joules. Whoever fits the name is whoever fits the energy bill and the bank account. The centre trains; the margin consumes — if quota remains, if English remains, if the network remains.

A model defined by size promises the same to everyone and delivers first to those who were already large.

## What “universal” names

*Universal* measures reach: every language as origin, not as late translation; every margin as a first-class user; every inference with a visible energy account. The ULM need not be the biggest. It needs to fit — in a pocket, on a mesh, in the language of whoever wakes it.

Large is a quantity. Universal is an obligation.

## What does not change

It is still a language model. It can still run locally. It is still software, with a licence, authorship and history. We do not call universal that which only speaks the centre’s language and spends a whole power plant to finish a sentence.

What changes is the done-when: a ULM that does not serve the person on the periphery — of any nation — is not yet universal. It is merely large.

## Law of this folder

From this paper on, what we mean to build is called ULM. We write LLM only to name the concept we refuse.

The house was already rehearsing the practice: trilingual by conception, energy sieve, local-first. Here the rehearsal becomes a name.
