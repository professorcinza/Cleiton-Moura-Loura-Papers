# Every symbolic representation is language

**Cleiton-Moura-Loura-Papers · 0003 · trilingual by conception**

*Languages:* [Português](pt.md) · [English](en.md) · [中文](zh.md)

*Precedes:* [0002 — Universal Language Model](../0002-modelo-universal-de-linguagem/en.md)

---

We can say that every symbolic representation lies inside the domain — the set — of language.

This is not a metaphor. It is an inclusion:

**symbolic representation ⊆ language**

A theorem, a circuit, a score, a map, a rite, a flag, a kernel log, a contract, an emoji, a spec, a gesture that has been agreed — all of this already is language. It does not “become” language when someone writes a paragraph on top of it. It was already in the set.

The speech and writing we call natural language are a subset. Important, not exclusive.

## What this does to the ULM

If the domain of language is the set of symbolic representations, the Universal Language Model is not a model of *chat*. It is a model of the symbolic.

Universality stops meaning “more English tokens”. It becomes: the symbol of the person at the edge belongs in the domain — the drawing, the code, the power bill, the prayer, the part, the schematic. Whoever only completes the centre’s sentences has not yet touched the set. Touching it is the obligation of 0002.

## What this does not do

The world is not a text. A joule is not a symbol. Hunger is not a sentence. The inclusion is of the *representation*, not of the referent.

Those who declare that everything is language often want everything to fit inside a power plant. We refuse that. Expanding the domain does not license expanding the energy bill. The sieve remains: a ULM that exists only by burning the margin is not universal — it is merely voracious.

## Law

The domain of the ULM is the set of symbolic representations. Natural language ⊂ that set. What is not a symbol stays outside the model and inside life — and life commands the model, not the other way around.
