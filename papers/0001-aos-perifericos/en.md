# To those on the periphery

**Cleiton-Moura-Loura-Papers · 0001 · trilingual by conception**

*Languages:* [Português](pt.md) · [English](en.md) · [中文](zh.md)

---

I begin my work in this repository dedicated to all those on the periphery of every nation who, even in hardship, make the impossible possible.

Not to palaces. Not to flags. Not to those who already have a table, a microphone and a map.

To those at the edge — of a city, of a country, of a language, of a power bill — who invent anyway. Who turn lack into method. Who make room for what they were told would not fit.

This repository is the place of writing. The code lives elsewhere; here the thought is dated, public, named. Each text is born in Portuguese, English and Chinese at the same moment: whoever reads from the margin is not a translation. They are origin.

I speak for no nation. I speak for myself. A Brazilian citizen, with no mandate, beginning the work on paper — and dedicating it to those who were already making the impossible possible before I had a folder to keep the sentence in.

---

*Cleiton Moura Loura*
*Brazil, 27 August 2026*
