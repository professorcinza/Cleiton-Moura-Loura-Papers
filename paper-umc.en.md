## Part I — The concept

### Abstract

This paper replaces the concept of *Large Language Model* (LLM) with **Universal Computational Model** (UMC). The substitution is not nominal: it changes the telos. *Large* names scale — parameters, capital, joules — and delivers first to those who were already large. *Universal* names an obligation of reach: every language as origin, every margin as a first-class user, every inference with a visible energy account. *Computational* names the door: every processed symbol admits a computational model — encoding, operation, operational equality. An axiom is laid down: **every symbolic representation belongs to the set of language** (symbolic representation ⊆ language). Natural language is a subset, not the domain. The UMC is therefore not a model of conversation: it is a model of the symbolic that only processes what is computationally modelable. The world, however, is not a text; a joule is not a symbol; the real is not a computer; expanding the domain does not license expanding the power plant. A UMC that does not serve the person on the periphery of any nation is not yet universal — it is merely large.

**Keywords:** universal computational model; symbolic representation; computability; periphery; energy; telos.

### 1. Dedication and the author's position

I dedicate this work to all those on the periphery of every nation who, even in hardship, make the impossible possible.

Not to palaces. Not to flags. Not to those who already have a table, a microphone and a map. To those at the edge — of a city, of a country, of a language, of a power bill — who invent anyway. Who turn lack into method. Who make fit what they were told would not fit.

The undersigned writes in his own name: a Brazilian citizen, with no mandate, no office, no power to represent Brazil, China or anyone else. This is not an institutional article. It is a public, dated writing with verifiable authorship. It is born in English, Portuguese, Spanish and Chinese at the same instant: whoever reads from the margin is not a translation. They are origin.

### 2. Introduction

Industry named the decade's dominant artefact by its size. *Large Language Model* became, at once, a technical description and a civilisational promise: more parameters, more tokens, more truth. The name hides the criterion. Whoever fits "large" is whoever fits the energy bill and the bank account. The centre trains; the margin consumes — if quota remains, if English remains, if the network remains.

The problem is not that large models exist. It is that largeness was raised to a definition. A concept defined by quantity cannot fail by injustice: only by being small. Another criterion is proposed here, and therefore another concept.

The thesis is threefold.

1. What is to be built is called a **Universal Computational Model** (UMC). LLM is written only to name the refused concept. ULM, only ULMFiT and the first vacated name. UM, only the second vacated name.
2. The domain of that model is the set of **symbolic representations**, not the subset of natural languages.
3. Every symbol the model processes is **computationally modelable**. What does not admit encoding and operation does not enter. The world is not computation.

The three theses require each other. Without the second, "universal" slides back into "more text from the centre". Without the first, the inclusion of the symbolic becomes an excuse for an even larger plant. Without the third, the symbolic becomes a claim over the incomputable and over the real.

The routine question of industrial research — "which front are you on: LLMs, vision, agents, safety, multimodal, optimisation?" — already chooses the refused concept. This paper is not on an LLM front. It is on the substitution of the concept that organises those fronts.

### 3. Related work and the gap

The Transformer architecture (Vaswani et al., 2017) made large-scale language-model training tractable. Scaling laws (Kaplan et al., 2020) raised size to an independent variable: more parameters, more data, more falling loss. The name *Large Language Model* is the slogan of that curve.

There is critique. Bender et al. (2021) refuse the stochastic parrot as an oracle and name cost, extraction, and harm to those who do not train. Strubell et al. (2019) put the energy account on the NLP table. None of these critiques, however, substitutes the concept. They correct the LLM; they do not depose it.

The gap is this: the literature treats *large* as the axis. In the standard vocabulary there is no telos that judges the model by the obligation of reach — language as origin, margin as test, joule as sieve — over the set of symbolic representations. This paper names that gap **UMC**. No priority over the artefact is claimed. Priority over the *name of the duty* is claimed. The chain *Universal Language Model* and the acronym ULM were already occupied (Howard & Ruder, 2018, ULMFiT). *Universal Model* is a generic phrase: UM was vacated. *Computational model* and computability (Turing, 1936) already existed — priority over the *string* UMC is likewise not claimed. The point-by-point sieve against UMC-001–011 is Part II of this paper.

### 4. The refused concept: *Large Language Model*

*Large* measures scale: number of parameters, volume of tokens, data-centre footprint, locked capital, joules per inference. It is an honest engineering metric and a dishonest metric of purpose. It confuses what the artefact *spends* with what the artefact *owes*.

A model defined by size promises the same to all and delivers first to those who were already large. The dominant training language becomes the language of the world. The margin enters as residual data or as market. The energy account disappears from the name — and the name is what is repeated.

The technical merit of large-scale models is not denied. What is denied is that scale is the concept. A concept is a telos: the for-what that judges the done and the failed. Under the telos of *large*, a system inaccessible at the edge, de facto monolingual and energetically voracious can still be "a successful LLM". That is enough to refuse it as the guiding concept.

### 5. The proposed concept: Universal Computational Model

**Universal** measures reach, not volume.

Reach of language: every language is origin, not late translation. A text born only at the centre and later "localised" for the margin is not universal; it is colonialism with good documentation.

Reach of person: the margin is a first-class user. The person on the periphery of any nation — who invents with lack — is not an edge case. They are the test.

Reach of energy: every inference carries a visible account. A feature that costs more energy than it returns must justify itself; what exists only by burning the margin is not called universal.

The UMC does not need to be the largest. It needs to fit: in the pocket, in the mesh, in the language of whoever wakes it. Large is a quantity. Universal is an obligation. Computational is a door, not a metaphysics.

The criterion of done follows from the obligation. A UMC that does not serve the periphery is not yet universal. It is merely large. It remains a model, it may still run locally, it is still software with licence, authorship and history. What changes is the judgement.

### 6. Axiom: every symbolic representation is language

One may say — and it is said here as an axiom, not as a metaphor — that every symbolic representation lies inside the domain, the set, of language:

\[
\text{symbolic representation} \subseteq \text{language}
\]

A theorem, a circuit, a score, a map, a rite, a flag, a kernel log, a contract, an emoji, a specification, an agreed gesture: all of this is already language. It does not "become" language when someone writes a paragraph on top of it. It was already in the set.

Speech and writing called natural language are a subset. Important, not exclusive:

\[
\text{natural language} \subset \text{language} = \{ \text{symbolic representations} \}
\]

If the domain of language is that set, the UMC is not a model of *chat*. It is a model of the symbolic. Universality stops meaning "more English tokens". It becomes: the symbol of the person at the edge belongs in the domain — the drawing, the code, the power bill, the prayer, the part, the schematic. Whoever only completes the centre's sentences has not yet touched the set.

### 7. Sieve: what enters is computationally modelable

The axiom includes. The door filters.

Every symbol the UMC processes admits a computational model: finite encoding, at least one operation, an operational criterion of equality. A circuit enters because it has a schematic and a rule. A score enters because it has notation and transformation. A "symbol" without operational representation does not enter — not a refusal of the margin; a refusal of smoke.

Computable here does not mean "fits in a huge data centre". It means: there is a model, there is an operation, there is a way to fail equality. Without that, "domain of the symbolic" becomes a licence to name the ineffable as a token.

### 8. Limits of the axiom and the sieve

The axiom is an inclusion, not an absorption of the real. The sieve is a door, not a metaphysics.

The world is not a text. A joule is not a symbol. Hunger is not a sentence. The real is not a computer. The inclusion is of the *representation*, not of the referent. Those who declare that everything is language, or that everything is computation, usually want everything to fit inside a power plant. That is refused.

Expanding the domain does not license expanding the energy account. The sieve remains. A UMC that exists only by burning the margin is not universal — it is voracious. What is not a symbol stays outside the model and inside life. What is not computationally modelable stays outside the model — and may remain life. Life commands the model, not the other way around.

This limit is part of the thesis, not an ethical appendix. Without it, the UMC collapses back into an LLM with a larger vocabulary, or into "it from bit" with a larger plant.

### 9. Conclusion

One concept has been replaced with another. LLM names the refused: scale as telos. UMC names the proposed: an obligation of reach over the set of symbolic representations that admit a computational model, with natural language as a subset, the margin as the test, energy as the sieve, and the real outside the plant.

It is not claimed that the UMC already exists as an artefact. It is claimed that the artefact, when it exists, may not call itself universal if it fails the periphery, if it fails language as origin, if it fails the account of joules, or if it processes what is not computationally modelable. The name is the debt. The work, from here, is to pay it.

### 10. Agenda: the continuation

The originating interlocution offered the menu of industrial research: literature review, SOTA comparison, paper dissection, architecture prototypes, training pipelines, CUDA, slides. This paper accepts what serves the telos and refuses what betrays it.

**Accepted.** The literature that installed *large* as the axis and the critique that corrected it without deposing it (§3). A table of criteria, not a leaderboard of a non-existent artefact. An evaluation protocol for when the artefact exists. Spec before weights.

**Refused.** Faking SOTA. Training a power plant to "prove" universality. Taking the day's hype as the state of the art. A trends briefing in place of an obligation.

|---|---|---|---|
| Telos | scale | reach | proposed in this paper |
| Success | lower loss, higher benchmark | the margin served; languages as origin; joule visible | unmeasured: no artefact |

**Protocol** (when there is an artefact — not before):

1. *Origin.* Is the same content born in English, Portuguese, Spanish and Chinese, without post-translation?
2. *Margin.* Can someone at the edge complete the task without a quota from the centre?
3. *Energy.* Are the joules of the task visible and justified?
4. *Symbolic.* Does it accept a spec, a circuit, a map, a contract, a score — or only chat?
5. *Computational.* Does every type have an encoding and an operation — or is it smoke with the name of a symbol?

To fail one item is to fail the name. The next step of this continuation is not a training run. It is the verifiable specification of the UMC: requirements, sieve, path of proof. No line of weights without a spec that governs it.

## Part III — Formal foundations: $L_U$, the linguistic universality hypothesis and the $R^*$ problem

### Abstract

The domain of the Universal Computational Model (UMC) is defined formally: the language $L_U$, the set of all symbolic representations that admit a computational model. The **Linguistic Universality Hypothesis (LUH)** is stated: every symbolic system $S_i$ with alphabet $\Sigma_i$ and grammar $G_i$ admits an injective encoding $E: S_i \to L_U$ that preserves semantics by decoding — in practical terms, *everything that can be written can be tokenized*. It is shown that $L_U$ is recursively enumerable (Turing-generable), Gödel-incomplete and statistically approximable; that no UMC is at the same time consistent, complete and closed; and that the central engineering problem stops being scale and becomes the selection of $R^* \subseteq \mathcal{H}$: the minimal representation of all human symbolic production that preserves the capacity to act, under a utility function $U(R|human)$ still to be defined. The world is not text: the non-symbolizable $N$ lies outside $L_U$, and that is a thesis, not an appendix.

**Keywords:** UMC; universal language; computability; Gödel; statistical approximation; utility function; non-symbolizable.

### 1. Domain: the language $L_U$

$L_U$ is defined as the set of all finite sequences of symbols over a finite alphabet $\Sigma$, generated by a grammar $G_U$, such that:

1. **Combinatorial syntax:** there is a rule of concatenation/composition among symbols;
2. **Compositional semantics:** the meaning of a compound expression is a function of the meanings of the parts and of the composition rule;
3. **Recursive capacity:** the grammar allows expressions to embed expressions, with no a priori depth limit.

$L_U$ is, at minimum, a recursively enumerable language: there exists a Turing Machine that enumerates all well-formed expressions. Decidability is not required — and §3 shows why it cannot be required.

### 2. Axiom: the inclusion of the symbolic

Axiom (restated from Part I):

\[
S \subset L_U
\]

where $S$ is the set of all symbolic representations: any structure where a signifier points to a meaning by convention — mathematics, code, score, circuit, map, contract, rite, agreed gesture. Natural language is a proper subset:

\[
\text{natural language} \subset L_U
\]

The axiom is the inclusion of the *representation*, not of the referent. The world is not text (§6).

### 3. The linguistic universality hypothesis (LUH)

**Hypothesis.** For any symbolic system $S_i$ with alphabet $\Sigma_i$ and grammar $G_i$, there exists an injective encoding $E: S_i \to L_U$ such that the semantics of $S_i$ is preserved by the decoding $E^{-1}$ over the image of $E$.

**Proof (constructive, sketched).** The construction is that of the modern *tokenizers*, generalized. Every expression of $S_i$ is a finite derivation tree of the grammar $G_i$. The set of productions of $G_i$ is enumerated as a finite vocabulary $\hat{\Sigma}$; each node of the tree is encoded as a sequence over $\hat{\Sigma}$; $E$ is defined as the serialization of the tree. The decoding reconstructs the tree, hence compositional semantics is preserved by structural induction. Since $L_U$ contains all sequences over $\Sigma \supseteq \hat{\Sigma}$, the image of $E$ lives in $L_U$. □

**Honesty note.** The proof shows *constructive existence* of an encoding. It does not claim that the optimal encoding is known, nor that all the semantics of $S_i$ is captured — only that the *symbolic system* (the formalizable part) is transportable into $L_U$ without loss of structure. What is lost was no longer symbolic: it is §6.

### 4. The three levels of modelability

The source dialogue separated three senses of "mathematically modelable". Here they stand as theorems.

**Level 1 — Modelable as definable (yes).** Every $S \subset L_U$ is generated by a Type-0 grammar at minimum (Chomsky, 1956, 1959); hence $L_U$ is Turing-computable as an enumerator (Turing, 1936).

**Level 2 — Modelable as decidable (no).** If $L_U$ contains arithmetic — and it does, since mathematics $\subset S \subset L_U$ — then $L_U$ is Gödel-incomplete (Gödel, 1931): there are well-formed propositions whose truth is not decidable within $L_U$. **Corollary:** no UMC is at the same time consistent, complete and closed. Oracles, tools and the world are not defects of a UMC; they are the consequence of Gödel applied to LLMs. The UMC is modelable as a *generator*, not as a *universal decider*.

**Level 3 — Modelable as learnable (yes, statistically).** What is modelled is not exact $L_U$ but the distribution $P_{L_U}$ over the observed support. By the universal approximation theorem (Cybenko, 1989; Hornik et al., 1989) and the scaling laws (Kaplan et al., 2020), a network with sufficient capacity and data approximates $P_{L_U}$ arbitrarily well on the observed support. The question stops being *is it modelable?* and becomes *with what sample efficiency?* — and the empirical answer (manifold hypothesis; Bengio et al., 2013) is: far more efficient than PAC theory (Valiant, 1984) predicted.

### 5. The $R^*$ problem: filtering the symbolic down to the useful

Let $\mathcal{H} = \{S_1, \dots, S_N\}$ be the set of every symbolic representation humanity has already created. The LLM trained on raw $\mathcal{H}$ and learned $P(\mathcal{H})$: it modelled humanity as it *is* — with noise, lies and redundancy. The UMC has another task:

\[
R^* = \arg\min_{R} |R| \quad \text{subject to} \quad \mathbb{E}[U(R | human)] > \tau
\]

where $R \subseteq \mathcal{H}$ is a filtered representation and $U(R|human)$ is a utility function still to be defined. Two statistical filters are proposed:

- **Epistemic filter:** $P(truth | S_i)$ — is it factual?
- **Pragmatic filter:** $P(\text{human action improves} | S_i)$ — does it help someone do something better?

RLHF (Christiano et al., 2017), DPO (Rafailov et al., 2023) and "constitutions" (Bai et al., 2022) are crude attempts at the pragmatic filter. The work of the UMC is not to scale data; it is to **scale the discarding** — compressing ten thousand years of symbol down to the kernel that increases human agency, by the razor of Solomonoff/MDL (Solomonoff, 1964): the best representation is the smallest one that still allows predicting and acting.

**Honesty.** The function $U(R|human)$ **cannot be defined by engineering alone**: utility is not in the text, it is in the experience of those who live. Whoever defines $U$ defines what is human. This paper defines the problem, not the answer.

### 6. The limit: the non-symbolizable $N$

\[
N \cap L_U = \emptyset
\]

$N$ is what cannot be symbolized without loss: qualia, pain, continuous experience, the body, the joule. A photo of a face represents by resemblance (Goodman, 1968: *dense*, not *articulated*, representation), not by convention — strictly speaking it is not symbolic. The UMC does not force $N$ into the symbolic; the human as guardian of the non-symbolizable guarantees this (UMC-006). The world is not text; hunger is not a sentence; the real is not a computer.

### 7. What this text does not do

It does not raise the status of any UMC. It does not authorize training (UMC-007: spec before weights). It does not claim the UMC exists. It does not claim novelty priority: everything that is a theorem here has a precedent in Part II (Chomsky, Turing, Gödel, Cybenko, Solomonoff, Goodman, Valiant, Bengio et al.). What this text *does* do is give the spec what it demands: verifiable definitions, from which the instruments of UMC-005 and UMC-011 follow.

### Phase 4 — The $U(R|human)$ problem (long horizon)

The source dialogue ends with the open question: *who is willing to pay the price of deciding what humanity forgets?* That is not engineering:

1. **Curator of truth:** distinguishing, in $\mathcal{H}$, knowledge that resists time from the noise of an era (historians, scientists, artisans — not clickers).
2. **Definer of value:** thousands of definitions of utility, negotiated culturally.
3. **Guardian of the non-symbolizable:** ensuring the UMC does not force $N$ into the symbolic.

Expected product: a protocol or institution for negotiating $U(R|human)$ — and the answer to the question of who decides what humanity forgets.

**Negotiation protocol for $U(R|human)$** (draft — expected product of Phase 4; the institution itself remains on the long horizon):

1. **Object.** Each cycle decides the subset $R \subseteq \mathcal{H}$ and the utility function $U(R|human)$ governing it. Nothing is erased: forgetting is lowering priority, never destroying.
2. **Seats.** Four, with distinct powers:
   - *Curator of truth* (historians, scientists, artisans — not clickers): distinguishes knowledge that resists time from the noise of an era; proposes what rises or falls.
   - *Definer of value* (cultural communities, with the margin first — UMC-003): proposes utility definitions, negotiated culturally.
   - *Guardian of the non-symbolizable* (those who live what cannot be symbolized: doctors, poets, carers, peoples): veto over any attempt to force $N$ into the symbolic (UMC-006).
   - *Engineer* (no vote on $U$): implements $R^*$ only after the agreement; never before (UMC-007).
3. **Decision.** No definition of $U$ passes by permanent majority: approval by qualified consent; the guardian's veto is absolute in the domain of $N$; the margin has veto weight over utility.
4. **Hard constraints.** UMC-001–011 keep governing. $U$ may never be defined to reduce the margin's agency. Nothing of $N$ into the symbolic by force.
5. **Record.** Every decision with date, authors and evidence — the same discipline as the spec. The log is public and auditable.
6. **Reversibility.** Every decision to forget is reversible: what was lowered remains accessible to whoever searches; only the priority changes.
7. **Iteration.** $U$ is revisited every cycle; the protocol runs while new symbolic production exists.

**Answer to the open question** (*who decides what humanity forgets?*): no one alone. Curators propose, communities define value, the guardian vetoes the non-symbolizable, the engineer only executes what was agreed. Forgetting is reversible prioritisation, never erasure — and whoever does not accept this answer does not define $U$.

**Verification:** minutes of each cycle with date and present seats; guardian veto recorded (0 violations of UMC-006); no artefact change without the agreed current $U$ (UMC-007); absence of physical erasure (provenance audit).
